BankPop currency symbols

Six candidates for one money mark across the app, drawn from the reference: flat cartoon, heavy dark outline, cream and gold. Each card shows the mark big, then at the sizes the app actually uses it - a balance chip, a table cell, and a running total.

01safest
Pop coin
A coin with a B stamped in it. Reads as money at any size and ties to the app icon.

Token

Every board currently carries its own symbol (₹, $, £, AED ...). One mark here would replace all of them for prices, balances, payments and the ledger.
