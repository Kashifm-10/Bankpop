One money sign for BankPop

A single character, in the same class as the signs below: it has to sit beside a figure at 13px in a ledger and still read at 34px in a balance. The eight below are built from the reference's bar-across-the-B idea, drawn as geometry rather than a font, so they render identically on every device.
